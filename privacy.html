Privacy Policy — Egodi Budget Assistant

Effective date: August 16, 2026

The Egodi Budget Assistant ("the Program") is a private, non-commercial SMS notification service operated by Adam Nahari for his own household. It sends budget and savings notifications to exactly two consenting recipients: the operator and his partner. It is not offered to the public.

Information we collect

  • Phone numbers of the two household recipients, provided directly by them with their express consent.
  • Household financial summary data (spending by category, budgets, and savings-goal progress) from the operator's own Monarch Money account. This is the household's own data about itself.
  • SMS replies sent to the Program's number, used to honor commands (such as PAUSE or STOP) and to personalize future messages.

How information is used

Information is used solely to generate and deliver the budget notifications described in the Program's terms, and to remember preferences and commitments the recipients express. It is never used for marketing or advertising.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party. The Program uses service providers strictly to operate: Twilio (SMS delivery), Anthropic (message drafting), and GitHub (system hosting). Each processes only the data required to perform its function.

Message frequency and rates

Recipients receive up to four (4) messages per day, sent at approximately 8 AM, 12 PM, 4 PM, and 7 PM Pacific time. Message and data rates may apply according to each recipient's mobile plan.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages permanently, or HELP for assistance. Recipients may also contact the operator directly at adamnahari@gmail.com.

Data retention and security

Message history and preferences are retained in the operator's private, access-controlled systems and deleted upon a recipient's request. Credentials and data are protected with industry-standard encryption in transit and encrypted secret storage.

Changes and contact

Any changes to this policy will be posted at this page. Questions: adamnahari@gmail.com.
