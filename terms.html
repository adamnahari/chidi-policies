SMS Terms & Conditions — Egodi Budget Assistant

Effective date: August 16, 2026

1. Program description

The Egodi Budget Assistant ("the Program") is a private, non-commercial SMS service that sends household budget notifications: daily spending summaries, budget-pace check-ins, alerts when a spending category exceeds its budget, savings-goal progress, and occasional large-transaction alerts.

2. Eligibility and consent

Participation is by direct invitation of the operator and limited to the two members of the operator's household, each of whom provided express prior consent. There is no public enrollment.

3. Message frequency

Up to four (4) messages per day, sent at approximately 8 AM, 12 PM, 4 PM, and 7 PM Pacific time. Some check-ins are skipped when there is nothing new to report.

4. Cost

The Program is free, but message and data rates may apply according to each recipient's mobile plan.

5. Keywords

  • STOP — permanently opt out of all messages.
  • HELP — receive assistance and the operator's contact.
  • PAUSE / VACATION — temporarily suspend messages.
  • RESUME — restart messages after a pause.

6. Not financial advice

Messages are automated informational summaries of the household's own budget data. They do not constitute professional financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

7. Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

8. Changes and contact

The operator may update these terms; changes will be posted at this page. Questions: support@egodi.app, or reply HELP.
